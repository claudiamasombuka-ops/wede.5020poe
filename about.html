Our Legacy & Mission

Rooted in Gauteng Soil

Founded near Pretoria in 1998, GreenAcres began with a clear mission: demonstrate that commercial agriculture can operate in perfect balance with nature. Over two decades later, our 150-hectare sanctuary produces over 45 organic crop varieties.

We combine traditional permaculture wisdom with modern green tech—such as rainwater harvesting and solar refrigeration—to supply Gauteng with wholesome, farm-fresh goodness.

Our Growing History

1998 — The First Seeds

Acquired 15 hectares in Kameeldrift, Pretoria, initiating organic soil restoration practices.

2008 — Certified Organic

Achieved full national organic accreditation and launched our local box delivery program.

2018 — 100% Renewable Transition

Installed off-grid solar arrays and a central reservoir drip system for sustainable water management.

2026 — Community Agricultural Hub

Welcoming over 5,000 visitors yearly for eco-tours, workshops, and farm-to-table culinary experiences.
